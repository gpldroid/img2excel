Important limitations

Disclaimer

Review the limitations of optical character recognition before relying on data extracted from an image.

Nature of OCR output

ProTools is an assistive tool for recognizing text and extracting table-like data from images. Automated OCR may produce incorrect, incomplete, duplicated, or misordered values. The extracted result is not a verified copy of the source document.

Factors that affect accuracy

  • Image resolution, sharpness, character size, and compression.
  • Lighting, shadows, glare, noise, and uneven backgrounds.
  • Page rotation, skew, perspective distortion, and cropping.
  • Unusual fonts, merged cells, faint borders, and complex layouts.
  • Document language, writing direction, punctuation, and table structure.

Review before use

Check every relevant cell, especially decimal values, currencies, dates, names, and identifiers. Do not rely on OCR output alone for financial reports, legal records, medical information, or administrative decisions. Keep the original image as the reference for verification.
