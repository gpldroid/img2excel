Data and privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ProTools handles images, browser settings, analytics, advertising, and third-party resources.

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Images and browser-based OCR

When you use the Tesseract OCR workflow, recognition runs in your browser using Tesseract.js and its configured language resources. The selected image does not need to be sent to a ProTools OCR server for this workflow. Browser libraries and language data may be downloaded from external content delivery networks. You remain responsible for the files you select and for reviewing extracted text.

2. Information stored in your browser

ProTools stores your light or dark theme preference in Local Storage. The application may also store a Google Analytics measurement ID if you choose to enter one in the app settings. Scan history is held in the current page session and is normally lost when the page is reloaded or closed. The current application does not require a user account or use a cloud database to store scan history.

3. Analytics and cookies

The website is configured with Google Analytics 4 using measurement ID G-NCECK5F16F. Google may process technical and usage information, such as browser and device details and page interactions, and may use cookies or similar identifiers under its service settings and applicable regional rules. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Some jurisdictions require notice or consent before non-essential analytics technologies are used.

4. Advertising

HTML pages include the Google AdSense library associated with publisher ID ca-pub-6996931662051674. Loading the library does not guarantee that ads appear on every page or confirm that all account and consent settings are complete. If ads are served, Google and its partners may process data and use cookies or similar identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted. Regional rules may require consent or a consent-management mechanism.

5. Third-party libraries and providers

The interface uses third-party resources such as Tailwind CSS, Lucide, Tesseract.js, SheetJS, Google Fonts, Font Awesome, and analytics or advertising services. When your browser requests these resources, providers may receive technical request data such as your IP address, subject to their own policies. Image handling depends on the selected processing workflow described above.

6. Email and support

If you contact info@larache.xyz, we process the contact details and message content needed to respond. Do not send passwords, API keys, or sensitive documents.

7. Your choices and rights

You can remove Local Storage data through your browser settings and choose not to enter a custom analytics measurement ID. Depending on your location, data protection laws may provide rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing.

8. Children's privacy

ProTools is not designed to collect personal information from children, and the core tool does not require account registration.

9. Policy updates and contact

This policy may change when product features or service providers change. For privacy questions, contact info@larache.xyz.
