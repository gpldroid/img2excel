How to use Image Format Converter

Choose one or more images, select PNG, JPEG or WebP as the output format, adjust quality for lossy formats and convert.

Supported workflow and limitations

The browser canvas encoder determines supported formats. Transparency is preserved in PNG and may be lost when converting to JPEG. Animated formats may be flattened to a still image.

Privacy and file handling

Your selected files are processed in your browser. ProTools does not require you to upload them to a document-processing server. Required JavaScript libraries and language data may be downloaded from their respective CDNs. Analytics and advertising are controlled separately by your cookie preferences.

Frequently asked questions

Can I convert transparent PNG to JPG?

Yes. JPEG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are rendered against a white background.

Will the output look identical?

PNG is lossless, while JPEG and WebP may use lossy compression. Choose a higher quality setting when visual fidelity matters.

Are images uploaded?

No. Conversion is performed in the browser on your device.
