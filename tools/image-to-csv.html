How to use Image to CSV Converter

Upload a clear table image, select its OCR language, and run recognition. The converter uses OCR line and word positions to estimate tab-separated columns. Review the grid and export CSV.

Supported workflow and limitations

OCR cannot guarantee accurate table boundaries. Low-resolution images, merged cells, rotated text and complex layouts can require manual editing. CSV does not retain cell formatting.

Privacy and file handling

Your selected files are processed in your browser. ProTools does not require you to upload them to a document-processing server. Required JavaScript libraries and language data may be downloaded from their respective CDNs. Analytics and advertising are controlled separately by your cookie preferences.

Frequently asked questions

How does it detect columns?

The converter groups recognized words by their vertical position and estimates columns from horizontal alignment. The resulting grid is a best-effort interpretation.

Can I recognize Arabic tables?

Yes. Choose Arabic as the OCR language and verify column order and cell contents in the editable preview.

Can I open the result in Excel?

Yes. Download the CSV file and open it in Excel or another spreadsheet application.
