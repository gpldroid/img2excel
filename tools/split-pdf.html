Split PDF

Extract selected pages into a separate PDF document.

Use the Split PDF

Ready. Files are processed locally in your browser.

How to use Split PDF

Select a PDF, enter page numbers or ranges such as 1-3,5, and create a new PDF containing those pages in the specified order.

Supported workflow and limitations

The tool extracts existing pages without changing their contents. Password-protected PDFs that the browser cannot open are not supported.

Privacy and file handling

Your selected files are processed in your browser. ProTools does not require you to upload them to a document-processing server. Required JavaScript libraries and language data may be downloaded from their respective CDNs. Analytics and advertising are controlled separately by your cookie preferences.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. Enter comma-separated pages and ranges, for example 1,3,5-7.

Does it change page quality?

No. Pages are copied into a new PDF without rasterizing them.

Can I split one PDF into several separate files?

This version creates one output PDF from the selected page list.
