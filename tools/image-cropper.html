How to use Image Cropper

Upload an image, set the crop position and dimensions, preview the result, and export it in your chosen image format.

Supported workflow and limitations

The crop is based on pixel coordinates and does not include advanced perspective correction, object removal or non-destructive layers.

Privacy and file handling

Your selected files are processed in your browser. ProTools does not require you to upload them to a document-processing server. Required JavaScript libraries and language data may be downloaded from their respective CDNs. Analytics and advertising are controlled separately by your cookie preferences.

Frequently asked questions

Can I crop to an exact size?

Yes. Enter the output width and height in pixels. The crop is limited to the source image bounds.

Does cropping reduce image quality?

PNG preserves pixel values. JPEG and WebP may introduce compression depending on the selected quality.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping is performed locally in your browser.
