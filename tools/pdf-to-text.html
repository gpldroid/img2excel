How to use PDF to Text Converter

Choose a PDF and select whether to extract all pages or a page range. The tool reads the PDF text layer in page order and provides a preview before downloading a TXT file.

Supported workflow and limitations

Scanned pages without a text layer may produce little or no output. Reading order can differ in multi-column documents, and text extraction does not preserve page design or images.

Privacy and file handling

Your selected files are processed in your browser. ProTools does not require you to upload them to a document-processing server. Required JavaScript libraries and language data may be downloaded from their respective CDNs. Analytics and advertising are controlled separately by your cookie preferences.

Frequently asked questions

Can it extract text from scanned pages?

No, not without OCR. Scanned pages are images. Use an OCR tool to recognize the text in those pages.

Does it keep the PDF formatting?

No. The output is plain text; fonts, columns, tables and page styling are not preserved.

Is the PDF sent to a server?

No ProTools upload is required. The browser reads the selected PDF locally.
