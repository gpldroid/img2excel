Advertising Information

This page explains the advertising technology configured on ProTools and the principles that guide ad placement.

Advertising status

ProTools includes the Google AdSense library with publisher identifier ca-pub-6996931662051674. Loading the library does not guarantee that an ad will appear on every page, and it does not by itself confirm account approval, consent configuration, or the presence of active ad units.

Placement principles

  • Advertising areas should be visually distinguishable from editorial content and application controls.
  • Ads must not obscure image upload, camera capture, processing, table editing, or export actions.
  • Ads must not be styled to resemble navigation links or functional buttons.
  • Ad delivery must follow Google policies and applicable privacy and consent requirements.

ads.txt

The site's ads.txt file should contain only the authorized seller record supplied by the publisher's Google account. Do not add unverified publisher or reseller identifiers.

Privacy and consent

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies for delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization where permitted. Review the Privacy Policy for details about third-party services and user choices.
