Contact ProTools

Send product questions, report a problem, or suggest an improvement to the ProTools workflow.

Email support

For support and general enquiries, email info@larache.xyz.

To help us investigate a technical issue, include the page URL, your browser and device, the steps that led to the issue, and the exact error message if one appeared. Do not send passwords, API keys, identity documents, or confidential source files.

What to include in a bug report

  • The browser name and version, plus whether you are using a phone or desktop.
  • The image format and approximate dimensions, if relevant.
  • The OCR language selected and the action that failed.
  • A short description of the expected result and what happened instead.

Before contacting support

See the Image to Excel guide for the normal workflow and the OCR accuracy guide for image-quality recommendations. You can also review our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
