About ProTools

ProTools is a browser-based productivity tool that helps turn tables in images into editable spreadsheet data.

Why we built ProTools

Re-typing information from screenshots, photographs, and scanned pages is repetitive and error-prone. ProTools is designed to shorten that workflow: prepare an image, run optical character recognition (OCR), review the extracted cells, and export a spreadsheet.

What the current tool can do

  • Upload an image from your device or capture one with a supported camera.
  • Adjust image orientation, brightness, contrast, and grayscale before recognition.
  • Recognize text with a broad catalog of Tesseract language packs, including English, Russian, and Arabic. Select up to three languages for mixed-language documents.
  • Review and edit extracted cells, add or remove rows and columns, and clean table data.
  • Copy table data and export it as an XLSX workbook.

How processing works

The current OCR workflow uses Tesseract in the browser. The selected image is processed by the browser rather than being sent to a ProTools OCR server. Required libraries and language data may be downloaded from their configured content delivery networks.

Accuracy and limitations

OCR is an assistive process, not a guarantee of exact transcription. Results depend on image sharpness, lighting, font size, language, table borders, skew, and document layout. The current product focuses on image-to-table extraction and XLSX export; it does not claim general PDF or Word document conversion.

Always verify names, dates, identifiers, decimal points, and amounts before using extracted information in business, legal, financial, or medical contexts.
