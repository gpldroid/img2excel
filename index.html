How to convert images into an Excel spreadsheet

ProTools is a practical image-to-Excel tool that uses in-browser optical character recognition (OCR) to turn pictures of tables, receipts, attendance sheets, and financial documents into editable spreadsheet data. Capture or upload an image, review the extracted rows and columns, make corrections, and export the result.

1

Capture or upload an image

Use your device camera or upload an image (JPG, PNG, or WebP) from your computer or phone.

2

Recognize and organize the data

Tesseract OCR recognizes text in your image. The extracted content is arranged into rows and columns for review.

3

Edit and export to Excel

Edit cells, normalize digits, and export your spreadsheet as an XLSX file.

Frequently asked questions about OCR and the converter

How can I get the best OCR results from an image?

Use a sharp, well-lit image and keep the document flat and upright. If needed, adjust contrast and brightness with the image enhancement controls before running OCR.

Are my uploaded images and documents private?

The current OCR workflow runs in your browser using Tesseract. Images are processed locally for recognition. Scan history is stored in your browser until you clear it. Review the Privacy Policy for details about analytics and third-party services.

Which image formats can I convert to Excel?

The tool supports common image formats including JPG, PNG, WebP, and BMP, as well as direct capture from a mobile or desktop camera.

Can I convert Arabic-Indic digits to standard digits?

Use the Normalize Numbers control above the table to convert Arabic-Indic digits into standard Western digits for easier spreadsheet calculations.

Does OCR work without an internet connection?

Tesseract recognition runs in your browser after the OCR library and language data have loaded. The first use may require an internet connection to download these resources. Once cached, availability depends on your browser cache and service-worker behavior.
